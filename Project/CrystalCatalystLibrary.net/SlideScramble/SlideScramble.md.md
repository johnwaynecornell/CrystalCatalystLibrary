## Documentation Notes

The articles serve different purposes: some explain implemented behavior, others examine mathematical properties, and others preserve experimental ideas.

Human-authored solutions and AI-generated analyses should retain clear attribution. Mathematical claims should distinguish observed behavior, computational verification, and general proofs.

The index is maintained as the documentation collection grows.
