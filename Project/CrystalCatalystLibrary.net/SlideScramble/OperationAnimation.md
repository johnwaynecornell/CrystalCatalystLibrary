# Operation animation

Portable Lens operations support three presentation styles: `Instant` commits immediately, `Primitive` animates the complete ordered `RecordedMove` expansion, and `Logical` animates the resulting local tile permutation. The command menu exposes all three plus `Automatic`, which chooses a logical animation for a directly counted operation when its permutation is supported, and otherwise animates the authorized primitive expansion.

Animation is presentation-only. `OperationAnimationPlan` captures the starting board, computes each intermediate board from the stored primitive rotations, and derives logical source/destination paths from tile identities in the final permutation. The live grid and recording controller are untouched while frames render. On completion the window rechecks the board, controller, mode, and position, then calls the existing operation commit once; move cost, recording, and victory handling remain on that boundary. Escape or focus loss cancels without committing.

Logical wrapped paths use the shortest toroidal displacement; primitive paths follow the recorded rotation direction. Both render clipped copies at the opposite board edge. Tile images and borders scale as one rounded visual frame; the lens symbols remain in the overlay above the moving tiles. Classic row/column dragging keeps its existing presentation path.

Standalone Slide rotations use the same `0.62` second cadence as logical Portable Lens animations. Multi-step operations retain their `0.16` seconds per primitive step, and user drag snapping keeps its existing timing.

Playback media steps and autoplay advance or reverse one recorded operation at a time; the displayed position remains a primitive-move offset. Animation uses the saved rotations, never the current resolver, and honors the presentation preference. If playback is already inside an operation, the next step animates its remaining primitives to the nearest boundary. Unsupported or incomplete logical transformations use primitive animation; Escape cancels the animation and pauses autoplay without changing the board or offset.

The saved primitive moves determine the stationary corner and the edge route during logical Shuffle playback, including for historical recordings. The diagonal mover stays full-size: one copy exits a lens edge while another enters simultaneously from another edge. Both are clipped to the lens cells, including when the lens wraps around the board; a different tile shrinks to bypass the center. Rule-aware generated challenges retain each verified operation plan and animate supported Shuffle and Swap operations as one logical transformation by default. Each completed animation commits the plan's exact stored primitive expansion, without scoring or recording the scramble, and the inverse solution is attached only after verifying the resulting board. Explicit Instant and Primitive preferences remain available.

The verified `ColumnSwap` and `RowSwap` expansions produce two simultaneous tile exchanges (four moving tile identities). Logical motion partners are derived from the actual permutation, rather than assuming an unverified single pair or substituting a simplified transformation.
